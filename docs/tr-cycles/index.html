Pipeline, run in your browser when you click “Run calculation”: uniform resampling → Savitzky–Golay smoothing (order 3) → linear detrend → Hann periodogram → peak picking (prominence ≥ 2 % of the maximum) → phases from the sign of the derivative, phases shorter than the minimum merged. Bands (kyr): precession 15–30, obliquity 30–55, short eccentricity 80–130, long eccentricity 360–450.

A GDE map from a few wells

Give the depositional environment interpreted at one or several wells and the depositional system you expect. The conceptual model draws the belts, the wells calibrate them — the way a GDE map is drawn by hand — and every well is checked: honoured or not, and how far from a boundary.

Atlas pages: EOD and facies maps
The same interval as an EOD map — a few environments of deposition with smooth curved boundaries and their names inside — and as a facies map with deltaic lobes, fluvial channels, turbidite fairways, basin-floor fans, carbonate platforms and MTCs drawn on top, editable one by one.
A margin, source to sink
One system per domain — continental, coastal, shelf, slope, deep marine — chained along the profile with gradual transitions; rivers feed deltas, a canyon in line with a delta feeds a basin-floor fan.
Wells, seismic, concept
The three inputs of a hand-drawn GDE map: the wells calibrate, seismic attribute layers steer the belts and give channels, reefs and faults, the depositional concept is drawn with editable key lines, polarised sources and isolated bodies.
Conceptual belts
17 systems: fluvial, aeolian; tide-, wave- and river-dominated deltas; shoreface–shelf, rimmed platform, ramp, mixed shelf, glaciomarine; mixed, sand-rich and mud-rich fans, slope apron; rift lake, playa — or your own belts. Widths and typical scales from the literature, sources listed.
Calibrated on the wells
Boundaries placed between wells of successive belts; transport direction and source fitted automatically.
Natural boundaries
Seeded smooth irregularity, reproducible; optional local warping for a well that breaks the order.
Ready for the project
PNG, IRAP lines for Petrel, GeoJSON (EOD and facies levels), polygons CSV, ZMAP+ grid of belt codes, wells check table.

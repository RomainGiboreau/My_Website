## Chess

I play. Not professionally, but seriously enough to have a rating and to lose sleep over positions I should have drawn.

What keeps me at it is the same thing I like about modeling: you build a picture from incomplete information, you commit to it, and the position tells you soon enough whether you were right. There's no partial credit for a plausible-looking plan.

Chess also teaches something that transfers directly to technical work — the difference between a move that looks strong and a move that survives the opponent's best reply. Most bad models are the first kind.

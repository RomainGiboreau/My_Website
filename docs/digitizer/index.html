1Image

Open a file, drop it on the canvas, or paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V. The image never leaves your browser.

2Axes

Click two points on the X axis, then two on the Y axis (ticks or grid lines), then type their values. Axes may be slightly rotated.

3Points

Click to add a point (drag to fine-tune before releasing). Drag an existing point to move it. Delete removes the selected point, arrows nudge it (Shift = 10 px), Ctrl+Z undoes. Hold Space or use the middle button to pan; the wheel zooms.

4Auto-extract

no colour

Pick the colour on the curve, adjust the tolerance until only the curve is highlighted, optionally draw a Region around the plot area, then extract.

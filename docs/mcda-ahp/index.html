2Reading the result

Each cell states how much more important the ROW criterion is than the COLUMN criterion: 1 equal, 3 moderate, 5 strong, 7 very strong, 9 extreme, 2, 4, 6, 8 in between, 1/3 means the column is moderately more important. The lower triangle is the reciprocal.
CR = CI / RI with CI = (λmax − n)/(n − 1). Below 0.10 is the usual acceptance limit. Above 10 criteria no verified random-index table is used: CR is shown as n/a.
